Live Air Quality, Based on Real Data

AERIS Health uses the World Air Quality Index (WAQI) network to fetch current AQI and pollutant readings for a location - no invented numbers.

  • AQI, PM2.5, PM10, CO, NO₂ and SO₂ readings
  • Live AQI based on the nearest available monitoring station
  • Missing pollutant values are shown as unavailable, never guessed

Health Risk That's Personal to You

The same AQI affects people differently. AERIS Health combines the current pollution level with your age, health condition, smoking status and exposure time into one risk score.

  • Risk percentage and risk level (Low, Moderate, High, Critical)
  • Possible affected organs and systems
  • This is a health-risk awareness tool, not a medical diagnosis
